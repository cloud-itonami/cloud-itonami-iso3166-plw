# Operator guide — PLW

Portal: no dedicated e-procurement portal was located -- procurement
opportunities are published directly on the government's own
`palaugov.pw/rfp` (Request for Proposals) and `palaugov.pw/ifb`
(Invitation for Bids) pages, regulated by Palau National Code Annotated
(PNCA) Title 40 Chapter 6 (Statutory Framework for National Government
Procurement). Entity: business license (PNCA Title 40 §1501, Bureau of
Revenue and Taxation) and a business identification number (PNCA Title
40 §1504, Revenue and Tax Office). Foreign-invested engagements
additionally require a Foreign Investment Approval Certificate (FIAC)
from the Foreign Investment Board (Foreign Investment Act, PNCA Title
28 §§103-108) and, when the declared business sector falls within the
Foreign Investment Act's own §105 reserved-sectors gate, clear that
sector's own ownership-composition requirement (at least one citizen
owner for §105(a) sectors, or all-citizen ownership for §105(b)
sectors) before a filing can proceed. Engagements requiring Palau Goods
and Services Tax (PGST) registration (Palau Goods and Services Tax Act,
PNCA Title 40 §1201 et seq.) must have that registration verified.
Human-gated filing only.
