# Business model — PLW

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for Palau.

- Foreign Investment Act (Palau National Code Annotated (PNCA) Title
  28, Chapter 1, §§101-127; RPPL 3-34 as amended) -- Foreign Investment
  Board FIAC approval, plus a two-tier §105 reserved-sectors gate: six
  activities require at least one citizen owner, five different
  activities are reserved exclusively for all-citizen-owned enterprises
- Business licensing (PNCA Title 40 Chapter 15, §§1501-1506) -- Bureau
  of Revenue and Taxation, Ministry of Finance; cross-references
  Foreign Investment Act compliance in its own text
- Public procurement (PNCA Title 40 Chapter 6, "Statutory Framework for
  National Government Procurement," §§601-664) -- routed through the
  general Revenue-and-Taxation title rather than a free-standing
  Public Procurement Act
- Palau Goods and Services Tax Act (PNCA Title 40 Chapter 12, RPPL
  11-11, effective 2023) + Business Profits Tax Act (Chapter 14
  Subchapter II) -- replaced the historical Gross Revenue and Net
  Income Tax
- Reserved-sectors gate (Foreign Investment Act §105) -- independently
  recomputes whether a foreign-invested engagement's own declared
  business sector and ownership composition (has-citizen-owner? /
  all-owners-citizens?) actually satisfy that sector's own reservation
  tier

## Trust Controls

Any actual portal registration or filing submission requires
Market-Entry Compliance Governor clearance and always escalates to
human sign-off. A false or fabricated regulatory-requirement claim is a
HARD hold.
